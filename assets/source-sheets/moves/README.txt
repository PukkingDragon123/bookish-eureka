ELEMENTAL ATTACK MOVE ICONS

Nine crop-ready pixel-art sheets are included. Each sheet contains 10 original attack-move icons in a 5 x 2 layout with large spacing for easy cropping.

Background: bright magenta for easy removal or chroma-keying.
Style: simple low-resolution pixel art, chunky dark outlines, flat readable silhouettes, no heroes or characters.

The move names and left-to-right/top-to-bottom order are listed in elemental_attack_moves_manifest.txt.

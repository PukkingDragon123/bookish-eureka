# VFX source

`fx1.png` – `fx14.png` are the FX filmstrips from the **Superpowers "RPG Battle
System" asset pack**, created by **Pixel-boy** at **Sparklin Labs** and released
under **CC0** (public domain).

- Source: https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs (`rpg-battle-system/fx`)
- Pack page: https://sparklinlabs.itch.io/superpowers
- License: CC0 1.0 — attribution not required, but appreciated.

`tools/build-vfx.py` splits these strips into frames and packs `assets/ui/vfx.png`.
